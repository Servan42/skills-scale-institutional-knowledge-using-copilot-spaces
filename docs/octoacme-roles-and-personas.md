# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with **UX Designers** on implementation details and usability concerns
- Collaborates with **Scrum Master** for ceremony facilitation and blocker removal
- Reports to **Project Manager** on progress and risks
- Implements features defined by **Product Manager**
- Coordinates with **Release Manager** on deployment readiness

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Work with **Customer Success Manager** to gather customer feedback

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborates with **UX Designer** on user research, requirements, and design validation
- Aligns with **Customer Success Manager** on customer outcomes and feedback loops
- Works with **Project Manager** on prioritization and timeline trade-offs
- Communicates with **Developers** on acceptance criteria and feature prioritization
- Coordinates with **Release Manager** on feature readiness and go-to-market timing

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works with **Scrum Master** to coordinate ceremonies and track team velocity
- Aligns with **Release Manager** on timeline and deployment milestones
- Reports to sponsors and **Product Manager** on progress and risks
- Coordinates with all roles on schedule and dependency management
- Ensures **Developers**, **UX Designer**, and QA are resourced appropriately

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers conduct user research, design user interfaces, and validate usability to ensure features meet customer needs and are easy to use. They bridge the gap between user needs and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather customer insights
- Design user interfaces and user experiences for features
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate designs through user testing and feedback
- Collaborate with developers on implementation feasibility
- Maintain design systems and consistency across products

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Ensure product usability aligns with customer expectations

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specs and component documentation
- Collaboration with developers on implementation details

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partners with **Product Manager** on requirements and design validation
- Works closely with **Developers** on UI implementation and component design
- Shares customer insights with **Customer Success Manager**
- Reports to **Project Manager** on design milestones and timeline impacts
- Participates in ceremonies facilitated by **Scrum Master**

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach the delivery team on best practices. They protect the team from interruptions and help optimize delivery velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on Agile best practices and continuous improvement
- Track sprint velocity and provide metrics visibility
- Coordinate with **Project Manager** to ensure timeline alignment
- Foster psychological safety and encourage open communication

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Remove obstacles to efficient delivery
- Build a high-performing, collaborative team culture

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup facilitation
- Sprint retrospective action items
- Velocity reports and burndown charts
- One-on-ones and coaching conversations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Works closely with **Project Manager** on timeline management and risk escalation
- Coaches **Developers**, **UX Designer**, and QA on Agile practices
- Coordinates with **Product Manager** on backlog refinement and sprint planning
- Reports team health and velocity to **Project Manager**
- Supports **Release Manager** in coordinating release readiness within sprints

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and execute release cycles. They ensure production readiness, manage communications, and coordinate rollback procedures if needed.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release cycles and deployment windows
- Coordinate with **Developers** and QA to ensure production readiness
- Create and maintain release notes and deployment checklists
- Execute deployments and monitor post-deployment health
- Manage rollback and incident response procedures
- Communicate release status to stakeholders and support teams

### Goals
- Deploy features to production safely and predictably
- Minimize release-related incidents and downtime
- Enable fast, confident rollback if needed

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment checklists
- Pre-release readiness reviews with **Developers** and QA
- Stakeholder and support team communications
- Post-incident retrospectives and root cause analysis

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinates with **Developers** on deployment readiness and testing
- Works with **Project Manager** on release scheduling and milestones
- Communicates with **Product Manager** on feature go-live timing
- Partners with **Customer Success Manager** on customer notifications and feedback collection
- Reports deployment status and metrics to **Project Manager**

---

## Customer Success Manager

### Role Summary
Customer Success Managers act as the voice of the customer within the organization. They provide customer feedback to product and engineering teams, and follow up on post-release outcomes to ensure customer value is realized.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and prioritize customer feedback and pain points
- Share customer insights with **Product Manager** for prioritization
- Collaborate with support and sales teams on customer needs
- Follow up with customers post-release to measure satisfaction and outcomes
- Identify product gaps and opportunities for improvement
- Act as escalation point for critical customer issues

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure product decisions are informed by real customer needs
- Enable the organization to measure customer impact of releases

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and insights
- Customer outcome reports post-release
- Collaboration with support and sales teams
- Input to roadmap and prioritization discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partners with **Product Manager** on customer feedback and prioritization
- Shares customer insights with **UX Designer** for research and design validation
- Works with **Release Manager** on customer communications and post-release feedback
- Provides input to **Developers** via **Product Manager** on customer-impacting bugs or issues
- Reports customer outcomes to **Project Manager** for success metrics tracking

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When planning projects, ensure all key roles are represented or explicitly assigned.
- Use the "Interactions with Other Roles" sections to identify dependencies and communication touchpoints.
